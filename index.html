Retro Games Management System

This project is for CS340 Group 17 portfolio project. The team is made up of Jerome Cagado and Gabriel Jean-Bertrand

The Retro Games databse will help keep track of sales of videogames, inventory, and efficienty for Retro Games stores. Videogames are now selling digitally and it is harder to find retro games or hardcopies of video games. As a result, Retro Games has gained new customers and did not have a way to keep track of the new customers, the quantity of games, nor maintain efficieny while also providing great customer service. As a result this database was created. This database will keep track of Retro Games employees, customers, developers, sales, and their videogame catalog.
